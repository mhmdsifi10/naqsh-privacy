Naqsh: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Naqsh («نقش») is a puzzle game for Android. This page says what information the game uses and why.

What the game itself keeps

Your progress, your settings and the name you may choose to enter are stored on your device. The game has no accounts and we run no servers: we do not receive, see or store this information.

If Android backup is switched on for your device, Google may include the game's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is part of your Google account and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The game shows adverts through Google AdMob. To do that, Google's advertising software in the game collects and uses information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with adverts. Google uses it to show and measure adverts, to limit how often you see the same one, and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires your consent for personalised adverts, the game asks for it before any advert is shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your device's settings (Settings, Google, Ads). How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying "Remove ads" in the game stops the adverts between levels.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

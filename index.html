Naqsh: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Naqsh («نقش») is a puzzle game for Android. This page says what information the game uses and why.

What the game itself keeps

Your progress, your settings and the name you may choose to enter are stored on your device. The game has no accounts and we run no servers: we do not receive, see or store this information.

If Android backup is switched on for your device, Google may include the game's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is part of your Google account and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

No advertising

The game shows no adverts, contains no advertising software and does not read your device's advertising ID.

Purchases

The full version and packs of keys are bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded, and whether the full version is owned, so it can be restored on a new phone.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game sends no information about you anywhere; the only connection it makes is to Google Play, when you buy something or restore a purchase.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
